A "hack" is a short, focused sprint. You join a team, pick a real challenge from a church or ministry, and build a solution in 24 hours.

There are mentors to help, food to keep you going, and times of worship and prayer along the way. At the end, every team shares what they made, and some keep building with a ministry partner.
