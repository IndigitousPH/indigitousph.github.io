1. **Design a verse graphic** for your church's page.
2. **Write a short testimony** and share it with one friend.
3. **Shoot a 60-second video** about what Jesus means to you.
4. **Pray for one person** you talk to online, by name.
5. **Join your local network** and meet others who want to do the same.
