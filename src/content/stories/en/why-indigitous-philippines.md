The Philippines is one of the most online countries in the world. Filipinos spend hours every day on social media, messaging apps and games.

That means the **digital space is a mission field** — and the people who build and create for it are needed.

## What we want to see

- Every Filipino developer, designer and creator discovering their place in God's mission
- Churches and ministries equipped to use tech well
- Practical tools built by Filipinos, for Filipinos
